   - Worklog update
   - Next AppImage candidates

Important rules:
- Do not guess the cause of the icon issue without checking build path and artifact contents.
- Explicitly check whether GTK plugin fallback is relevant.
- Do not treat launcher icon success as proof that all in-app icons are fine.

- Do not mix both AppImage tracks into one huge session unless the change remains genuinely small and safe.
